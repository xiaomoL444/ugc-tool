## 音效播放器 更新 Sound Effects Player (SFX) Update

- 为播放器提供音频波形预览，通过音频波形预览可查看波形、拖拽进度
- 现在将鼠标移动至音效名称与音效ID上可单击复制音效名或音效ID



- Provides an audio waveform preview for the player, allowing users to view the waveform and drag the progress bar.
- You can now hover your mouse over the sound effect name or ID and click to copy it.
