## 角色技能动画查看器更新

- 本次新增 45 个动画预览
- 新增 37 个单段动画，其中普通攻击分类 28 个、持续施法分类 9 个。鼓掌、点头、打招呼、大笑等动作均可单独预览。

## Character Skill Animation Viewer Update

- This update adds **45 animation previews**.
- **37 single animations** are now available: 28 in **Normal Attack** and 9 in **Sustained Cast**. You can preview actions such as Clap, Nod, Greet, and Big Laugh individually.
- **8 combo animations** have been added, including Stand with Hands on Hips, Sit and Talk, Sit with Arms Crossed, and Think While Seated. Each preview plays the pre-cast, loop, and post-cast phases in sequence.
